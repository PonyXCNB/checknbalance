# Utah — the Lieutenant Governor's OWN candidate-status list (pulled Sept 2, 2026)

**Source: `https://vote.utah.gov/2026-candidate-filings/` — the page states "Last updated: 8/31/2026"
and "As of 3/16/2026, this page will be updated once weekly."**

⚠⚠ **THIS PAGE IS THE PRIMARY SOURCE AND IT BEATS EVERY AGGREGATOR.** It carries a `Status` column,
so it distinguishes who is actually on the November ballot from who filed and lost:
`Election Candidate` = ON THE NOVEMBER BALLOT · `Out in Convention` · `Out in Primary` ·
`Withdrew` · `Disqualified` · `Write-In`.

## ✅ THE VERIFIED NEGATIVE — NO STATEWIDE EXECUTIVE OFFICE IS ON UTAH'S 2026 BALLOT
The page's own section list is: **Federal Offices · State Senate · State House · State School Board ·
Judicial Retention.** There is a "State Offices" NAV LINK at the top, but **the document contains no
State Offices section at all** — no Governor, Lieutenant Governor, Attorney General, State Auditor,
State Treasurer, and **no U.S. Senate race**. Utah's entire statewide content in 2026 is ballot
measures plus appellate judicial retention.

## ✅ STATE SCHOOL BOARD IS DISTRICT-ELECTED, CONFIRMED
Every row reads "State School Board District N", including "(Multi-County)" markers and one
