HILLSBORO R-3 SCHOOL DISTRICT

PROPOSITION H


Shall the Board of Education of
Reorganized School District R-3 of
Jefferson County, Missouri, be
authorized to increase the operating tax
levy ceiling of the District by $1.39 per
one hundred dollars of assessed
valuation beginning with the 2027
assessment for general operating
purposes, constructing, improving,
renovating, furnishing, equipping and
operating facilities of the District,
including construction of a secured
entrance at the high school, installation
of security cameras and secured doors
throughout the District, playground
safety, security, and ADA upgrades,
construction and improvement of road,
parking, and traffic infrastructure to
improve student drop-off and pickup
operations and reduce traffic
congestion, improvements to athletic
and fine arts facilities districtwide,
construction of new high school fine arts
classrooms that also serve as a storm
shelter, and construction of a career and
technical education facility at Bridle
Ridge that also may serve as a
community storm shelter?

If this proposition is approved, the
adjusted operating levy of the school
district is estimated to be $4.7338
(based on 2025 tax rates) per one
hundred dollars of assessed valuation.

    YES
    NO

MERAMEC VALLEY R-III SCHOOL
DISTRICT
PROPOSITION K


For the purpose of renovating and
improving school buildings and facilities,
constructing, equipping, and furnishing
a new middle school with storm shelter,
installing safety and security updates,
and acquiring school buses, shall the
Board of Education of the Meramec
Valley R-III School District of Franklin,
St. Louis, and Jefferson Counties,
Pacific, Missouri, issue general
obligation bonds in the amount of
$45,000,000? Because previous debt is
being retired, if this proposition is
approved, the debt service levy of the
school district is estimated to remain
unchanged from the current debt
service levy of $0.95 per one hundred
dollars of assessed valuation of real and
personal property.

    YES
